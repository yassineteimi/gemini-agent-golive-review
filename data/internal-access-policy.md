# Internal access policy

Owner: Information Security, approved by the Chief Information Security Officer. Version 1.4, effective 1 October 2026. Applies to every employee, contractor and service account, including AI agents that read internal documents.

## Principles

Staff get the access their current job needs and nothing more. Access follows the role, not the person, so a move to a new team ends the old access. An AI agent acting for an employee sees only what that employee is allowed to see; it never gets broader access than the person asking the question.

## Roles and document access

| Role | Can read | Can't read |
|---|---|---|
| Branch adviser | Customer procedures, product sheets, their own branch's customer files | Other branches' customer files, HR files, audit reports |
| Contact centre agent | Customer procedures, product sheets, customer files opened for a live call | HR files, audit reports, credit committee minutes |
| Payments Operations | Card and payment dispute files, scheme rules, fraud alerts | HR files, credit committee minutes |
| Compliance | Every policy, complaint files, audit reports, retention records | Medical records held by HR |
| Human Resources | HR files for the employees they manage | Customer files |

A request outside this table goes to Information Security with a business reason and the manager's approval.

## Joining, moving and leaving

- **Joining.** The manager requests access through the access portal at least 3 business days before the start date. Accounts stay disabled until the first working day.
- **Moving.** Old access ends on the day of the move. The new manager requests the new access; the old manager confirms the old access can go.
- **Leaving.** Human Resources triggers the departure in the HR system and every account is disabled within 4 hours of the end of the last working day. For a dismissal, accounts are disabled before the employee is told.

## Privileged access

Administrator accounts are separate from day-to-day accounts and need multi-factor authentication on every sign-in. A privileged session on a production system expires after 8 hours. Nobody approves their own privileged access request.

## Access reviews

Every manager reviews their team's access each quarter in the access portal. Access that isn't confirmed within 15 business days of the review opening is removed automatically. Information Security reviews privileged accounts every month.

## Passwords and sign-in

Staff sign in with single sign-on and multi-factor authentication. Passwords are at least 14 characters long. Sharing an account, a password or a security token is forbidden and is treated as a security incident.

## Reporting a problem

Anyone who sees access they shouldn't have, or a colleague using someone else's account, reports it to the Information Security desk on extension 4400 or through the incident form. Reports can be anonymous.
